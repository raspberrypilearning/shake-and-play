### You will need

**For the wearable:**
- The cardboard template
- Thick cardboard
- Pencil
- Ruler if you are drawing the template by hand
- Tape
- 3 hair elastics
- Scissors
- Pens or craft materials to decorate (optional)

**For the micro:bit:**
- Micro:bit V2 and a USB cable
- Battery pack and 2 AAA batteries
