## Challenges

> [!TASK]
>
> ### Amplify the movement
>
> Use the multiply block{ADD!!!} to the tilt to increase the movement. Try different numbers instead of `2`.
>
> ```microbit
> (Rotation.Roll) * 2
> ```

> [!TASK]
>
> ### Use a different tilt
>
> Try `pitch`{:class='microbitinput'}, then tip the micro:bit forwards and backwards to change the tempo.

> [!TASK]
>
> ### Try a different sensor
>
> Add different sensors in the tempo, like `acceleration (mg) x`{:class='microbitinput'} or `light level`{:class='microbitinput'}. With acceleration, try `y` and `z` too.

> [!TASK]
>
> ### Make a melody
>
> Use the `play melody`{:class='microbitmusic'} block from the `Music`{:class='microbitmusic'} menu. Click the melody to open the editor and make your own sound.
>
> ![The melody editor open below a play melody block inside on shake. Choose squares to change the notes, then click Done.](images/melody.png){:width="450px"}
